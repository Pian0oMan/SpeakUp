How it works

Three steps from "I can't speak" to your first real conversation.

1

Pick your languages

Tell us what you speak and what you're learning. That's the whole setup.

2

Get matched, or join a room

Quick Match pairs you with someone who's learning your language. Or drop into a voice room with up to 10 people.

3

Hover, save, remember

Don't know a word? Hover over it. Save it to your deck, and practice it until the ring turns green.

Everything you need to really talk

Built for conversation first, and studying second.

Video and voice calls

Talk face to face, or keep your camera off. You decide when to turn things on.

Voice rooms

Group conversations for 3 to 10 people, with a side chat for when you'd rather listen.

Hover to translate

Every word and phrase can be translated in context, so you learn what it meant in your conversation.

Live captions

See what people say as they say it, and translate any spoken word.

Decks that track you

Every saved word gets a proficiency ring that fills up as you practice. Import your Anki decks, too.

Icebreakers

Never run out of things to say. A new question pops up every 90 seconds.
