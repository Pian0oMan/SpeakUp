How it works

From "I can't speak" to talking

Three steps to your first real conversation.

1

Pick your side

Speak English and learning Japanese, or the other way around? That's the whole setup.

2

Get matched, or join a room

Quick Match pairs you with someone from the other side, so you both get to practice. Or drop into a voice room with up to 10 people.

3

Hover, save, remember

Don't know a word? Hover over it. Save it to your deck, and practice it until the ring turns green.

Flashcards

Words from your chats become words you know

Tap a word you don't know and save it, together with the sentence you heard it in. Speak Up brings each card back right before you'd forget it.

  • From real conversationsEvery card remembers where you met the word, so it sticks.
  • Comes back at the right timeKnow it? See it again in a day, then 3 days, then a week... Forgot it? It's back in a minute.
  • Rings that fill upEach word's ring goes from red to green as you learn it.
  • Decks ready to goStart with a beginner deck for Japanese or English, or bring your own from Anki.

    Never run out of things to say. Every few minutes a new question pops up, often about something you both like.
